## 1. THE ANSWER

**Yes. Build the trip around Preveza / Aktion (PVK), and keep Saturday 12 – Saturday 19 June 2027.**

Three of your five markets — Germany, Netherlands, UK — reach Vassiliki in **~8–9 hours door-to-door with no ferry**, because Lefkada is the one Greek island you drive to (causeway + Agia Mavra floating bridge). PVK to Vassiliki is **57–65 km, 60–90 min** (plan 75). That is the single most decision-relevant fact in this brief and it is solidly verified.

**The mid-June worry that triggered this research is largely unfounded — but not entirely, and the real risks are not where anyone expected.** Using the direct analogue week (Sat 13 June 2026) as proxy, PVK had **~14 international arrivals on that Saturday**, covering every one of your top three markets:

> Jet2 LGW 05:55→11:15 · easyJet LGW 07:10→12:30 · Transavia AMS 05:10→09:00 · Condor DUS 05:50→09:40 · Condor MUC 06:05→09:10 · Austrian VIE 05:30→08:20 · Condor FRA 11:45→15:05 · Ryanair NUE 12:50→16:10 · BA LHR 13:40→19:10 · TUI fly NL AMS 14:00→18:15 · Jet2 MAN 15:35→21:05 · Eurowings DUS 15:55→19:35 · Eurowings STR 17:05→20:20 · Austrian VIE 17:40→20:30

Saturday is not a thin day at Preveza. It is the best day of the week for the markets that matter.

**Two markets you should not spend money on: Spain and Scandinavia.** Not because they're impossible — both are serviceable one guest at a time — but because the routing is 11–14 hours and the mid-June evidence is either unresolved (Nordics) or structurally absent (no Spain–PVK service has ever existed, in any month, ever).

## 3. SATURDAY vs TUESDAY — DIRECT ANSWER

**No. The flight reality does not argue for changing the shape. Kill Tue 15 – Sun 20.**

Four independent reasons:

1. **It is five nights, not seven.** You are giving up two riding days before a single flight argument is made.
2. **Tuesday is the thinnest UK day of the entire week** — two flights total, one of them London. No price competition, no fallback if it cancels.
3. **Sunday returns are broken for Germany.** Condor has no Sunday DUS or MUC rotation in either its 2026 or 2027 published plan. A shape whose return day serves Frankfurt and nobody else is not servable for a German group.
4. **Tuesday loses, relative to Saturday:** both Gatwick mornings, both Amsterdam rotations, Nuremberg, Munich, Manchester, and one of Vienna's two rotations — and gains essentially nothing that actually flies in mid-June (the Tue-only options — Eurowings' 2nd Stockholm frequency, Norwegian CPH — both start *after* your window).

**One earlier claim that has to be withdrawn:** the "Sunday is the volatile day" argument against Tue–Sun was built on 2024 data. In 2026 the Dutch Sunday ran fine in mid-June. Tue–Sun still dies, but on the four reasons above, not that one.

**For completeness, since it will be asked:**
- **Sunday–Sunday** is the only genuinely *better* day, and only for the UK — it is the sole shape that serves Birmingham, Bristol, Edinburgh, Luton and Leeds Bradford, and Jet2's new Leeds Bradford route (Sun, 23 May–10 Oct 2027, confirmed 2027 data) is Sunday-only. It does not beat Saturday for NL or Germany. Not worth the move.
- **Monday–Monday** is Nordic-optimal (Sunclass CPH, Sunclass OSL, Apollo ARN/Norrköping are all Monday). It would repair market #5 by breaking #1 and #2. Do not.

## 4. THE MID-JUNE SEASONALITY RISK — REAL, BUT NARROW

**There is no mid-June cliff at Preveza.** June 2025 ran ~158k passengers against August's ~204k — roughly 78% of peak, and level with September. October is 28% of August; *that* is what a shoulder month looks like. (Monthly Fraport figures were extracted directly by one reviewer and could not be re-fetched by another — treat as probable, not certain. PVK's 875,962 annual 2025 is independently confirmed.)

What *is* real is a **day-and-route** problem. These do not fly in mid-June and must not appear in any marketing:

### The two genuine hazards nobody flagged until late:

**(a) Condor asterisked Düsseldorf and Munich Saturday for 2027.** Condor's own S2027 flight plan (published 2 Aug 2026, valid 01.05.27–31.10.27) prints the Preveza row as:

```
S2027:  DUS Di*,Do*,Sa*  |  FRA Di*,Do,Sa,So  |  HAM Sa  |  MUC Di,Do,Sa*
S2026:  DUS Di*,Do*,Sa   |  FRA Di*,Do,Sa*    |  HAM Sa* |  MUC Di,Sa
```
Legend: `* = Kein ganzsaisonales Angebot` (not a full-season offering).

**Düsseldorf and Munich Saturdays each *gained* an asterisk for 2027**, having run full-season from 9 May and 2 May in 2026. Those are exactly the two catchments an earlier draft starred as the cheapest, best routings. The asterisk does not state a start date — but the 2026 precedent for an asterisked Saturday is Frankfurt, which started **2 June**, only ten days before your date. **Frankfurt and Hamburg went the other way and are unasterisked (full-season) for 2027.** This is a paperwork signal, not a cancellation, but it is the single highest-value thing to resolve.

**(b) Stuttgart's Saturday block ends in mid-June.** Eurowings STR–PVK Saturday ran **23 May – 20 June 2026**, then stopped for six weeks and resumed 1 August. On the 2027 calendar the equivalent block is roughly 22 May – 19 June — meaning your **outbound fits comfortably and your 19 June return sits exactly on the cutoff.** If Stuttgart guests matter, verify the block's *end* date, not its start. (Aegean's STR–PVK is a codeshare on this same flight, not extra capacity.)

## 5. WHO NOT TO MARKET TO

**Do not run paid acquisition into Spain or Scandinavia for this edition.**

**Spain.** Zero direct service to Preveza, from any Spanish airport, in any month, ever — this is permanent, not seasonal. The good news is that an earlier draft judged Spain far too harshly: it claimed Condor's Frankfurt Saturday starts ~20 June, making 12 June "the worst possible day." That is wrong by three weeks — the 2026 Saturday started **2 June**, and 2027 upgrades it to full-season. So **BCN → FRA → PVK on one Condor ticket, arriving ~15:05, is very likely available on Sat 12 June.** Vienna is the answer for Madrid: Austrian runs **two Saturday rotations** in mid-June (05:30 and 17:40), one ticket, Star Alliance, bags through — better than the Corfu-plus-ferry chain anyone recommended. Serve Spanish guests who come to you. Don't chase them: 10–14 hours door-to-door against 8 for a German guest, and the Barcelona plan hangs on Condor's FRA–BCN route (launched 1 May 2026) surviving into 2027.

**Scandinavia.** Downgrade all three countries to HARD. The Swedish case has collapsed: **Eurowings ARN–PVK did not operate in 2026** (FlightConnections states verbatim that no direct Stockholm–Preveza flights exist; the AeroRoutes schedule everyone leaned on is a 2025 file). FlightConnections' complete direct-origin table for PVK lists **no Stockholm, no Gothenburg, no Copenhagen** — the only Nordic direct is Oslo, and that starts 18 June. The charter programme (Ving, Spies, Apollo) is **Monday**, and ving.se currently shows 2027 Lefkas departures on 8 May and 22 May only — **no June 2027 date is on sale from any Nordic operator.** Nordic charters are invisible to GDS data so absence today is not proof of absence, but nothing establishes Saturday access from any Nordic capital in mid-June.

**Also do not market, within otherwise-good markets:** **Hamburg** (no mid-June service in 2026; the 2027 upgrade is on wet-leased Marabu metal and unproven), **Berlin** (no June direct), **Glasgow** (no route has ever existed), **Madrid** (route it, don't advertise it).

## 6. WHAT COULD NOT BE VERIFIED — SAID PLAINLY

**Only four genuinely reproducible 2027 facts exist across all of this research:**
1. Jet2 **Leeds Bradford–Preveza, Sundays, 23 May – 10 Oct 2027** — confirmed by three outlets. Sunday-only, so it serves neither of your shapes.
2. easyJet's 2027 schedule **windows**: Spring 22 Mar–13 Jun (released 16 May 2026), Summer 14 Jun–26 Sep (released 28 Jul 2026). **Your week straddles the seam** — 12 June is in the spring file, 19 June in the summer file. Check them separately.
3. Jet2 and TUI put S27 on sale Oct 2025; Transavia opened S27 sales 12 June 2026 with ~50% of capacity.
4. Condor's S2027 weekday grid (the asterisk table above).

**Everything else labelled "2027" is inference from 2026 patterns.** Specifically:

- **No fare in any of this research is reproducible.** The €213–280 German band, the €279 Düsseldorf open-jaw, the £120–350 UK bands, the Nordic package prices — all are single screen-scrapes from bot-blocked sites (Kayak, Eurowings, ving.se all return 403 to independent retrieval). **Do not put "flights from €X" in any copy until re-quoted from scratch.**
- **No 2027 flight times exist for any carrier.** Condor's S2027 PDF prints weekdays and asterisks only — no times, no dates, no season starts.
- **Unconfirmed:** whether AMS–PVK was in Transavia's 12 June 2026 S27 tranche (the whole Dutch case rests on it and it's a five-minute manual check); whether easyJet or Jet2 actually operate LGW–PVK on Sat 12 June 2027; BA's Heathrow Saturday day-of-week (single-aggregator only); whether any Nordic operator has a June 2027 Saturday at all.
- **The UK "34 flights/week" figure is overstated** — FlightConnections gives ~27 for the four main UK airports. Directionally right, precision unwarranted.
- **Two aggregators are poisoned and were the source of every propagated error found:** sailionian.com silently mixes 2025 and 2026 rows, and Wikipedia's Aktion page lists carriers that no longer fly (Discover, Lufthansa, Eurowings Stockholm). **Do not use either.** Use, in order: the airline's own timetable PDF → `info.flightmapper.net/route/<Airline>_<code>_<ORG>_<DST>` (prints per-flight effective from/through dates, best tool for this exact question) → FlightConnections for existence checks only.

**Ground-transport numbers are also softer than they look.** PVK→Vassiliki taxi quotes span **€50–65 to €150–190** — a 3x spread on a line item you pay twice per guest. Public transport is not a fallback: 4 buses/day airport→Lefkada Town, but only **twice daily** onward to Vassiliki (2h31 total). Every guest needs a pre-booked transfer. And if you ever route anyone via Corfu, add 20–40 minutes to every published estimate: the "50-minute ferry" everyone quotes departs from **Lefkimmi, 40+ km south of Corfu airport** — from Corfu Town it is 1h10–1h30, making the realistic no-car total ~6 hours, not 4.5. That strengthens the (correct) case for basing on PVK.

## 8. TWO OPERATIONAL NOTES BEFORE YOU COMMIT

**Nobody has costed the Saturday crush.** Jet2 LGW, easyJet LGW, BA LHR, Jet2 MAN, Transavia AMS, TUI AMS, Condor DUS, Condor MUC, Condor FRA, Ryanair NUE, Eurowings DUS, Eurowings STR and Austrian VIE all rotate through PVK on a June Saturday. It is a single-terminal regional airport. **Budget 45 minutes for bags, not 20**, book transfers well in advance, and check that hotel check-in and rental-gear handover in Vassiliki can absorb the whole group landing in two waves (a morning cluster ~09:00–11:15 and an afternoon/evening cluster ~15:00–21:00). Every draft treated Saturday density as pure upside. It is also the operational risk.

**The marketing claim survives full adversarial scrutiny — use it.** Two to three hours' flying. Roughly eight to nine hours door-to-door from London, Amsterdam, Düsseldorf, Munich or Frankfurt. **No ferry — Lefkada is reached by causeway.** That is true, it is verifiable, and it beats every Corfu-based windsurf competitor by about four hours. It is also true of no other Greek windsurf destination. Lead with it.
